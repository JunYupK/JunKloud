The application reads its settings from a file in the ConfigMap `orders-config`, which is mounted at `/etc/orders`. Today the Pod is not running: it keeps crashing and the Service has no endpoints. Find out why `orders` is not running and fix the root cause. Do not change the image or the command of container `app`.
